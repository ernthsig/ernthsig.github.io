Formula Student Low Voltage System

Design and manufacturing of the wiring harness and Brake System Plausibility Device (BSPD) for the Formula Student TUC 2023 race car.

Key Features

  • Vehicle wiring harness modification and sensor integration
  • Integration of critical safety systems and shutdown circuitry
  • Electrical schematic design and wiring optimization
  • Custom BSPD PCB design and assembly for throttle fault detection
  • Compliance with Formula Student safety regulations

Competition

  • Formula Student SAE Italy 2023
