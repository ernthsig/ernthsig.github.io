FMR Marine Platform

A modular autonomous marine platform designed for environmental monitoring, maritime security, search and rescue, and underwater operations.

Key Features

  • Custom-designed USV platform with modular architecture
  • AI-powered perception and autonomous navigation
  • Integrated debris collection and compaction system for water cleanup
  • Support for search and rescue and lifeguarding operations
  • Integration of tethered underwater vehicles (UUVs) with live video and sonar capabilities
  • Mobile command, communication, and power platform
  • Applications in maritime surveillance, disaster response, underwater infrastructure inspection, and environmental data mapping
