Water Surface Cleaning USV

Design, development, and manufacturing of an autonomous surface vessel for collecting floating debris within a geofenced environment, minimizing human intervention.

Key Features

  • Complete mechanical, electrical, and control system design
  • Custom catamaran hull designed in CAD and manufactured using 3D printing, fiberglass, and composite aluminum
  • Integration of Pixhawk 2.4.8 and ArduPilot for autonomous navigation and waypoint-based path planning
  • Geofenced autonomous operation for water surface cleanup
  • Development and testing of a fully functional proof of concept

Achievements

  • Fully funded by the Ideas Incubator Program
  • Awarded 1st Place at the 2024 International EUSAIR POPRI Student Competition in Croatia
