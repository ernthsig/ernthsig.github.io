I am a Production and Management Engineer (MEng), a graduate of the Technical University of Crete, with a particular interest in mechatronics, embedded systems, robotics, and motorsport.

My professional experience includes working as Lead Hardware Engineer at FluidMotion Robotics, where I was responsible for the development of redundant autopilot systems, safety critical electronics, and autonomous marine platforms. Previously, I worked as an R&D Engineer at Figure Eight, a technology startup focused on marina management, developing custom electronic boards, IoT sensor systems, and computer vision solutions.

During my university years, I served as Team Captain and Chief Engineer of Formula Student TUC, leading the development of the team's first race car and its participation in Formula SAE Italy 2023, while also being responsible for the vehicle's low voltage electrical and safety systems. My experience in the automotive industry also includes an internship as an Automotive Technician at Porsche Center Athens, where I gained practical experience in vehicle diagnostics, inspection, and maintenance.

Through these experiences, I have developed a multidisciplinary engineering background, combining electronics, software, mechanical design, and technical team leadership. I particularly enjoy bringing engineering concepts to life, from initial design and prototyping to manufacturing, system integration, and real world testing.
